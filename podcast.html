About the Show

Horror that lingers, not shocks

Ash & Iron the podcast was born from a belief that the most lasting horror lives in the mundane: the familiar made slightly wrong, the ordinary made irreversible.

These are not jump-scare stories. They are slow accumulations — the kind that you only realize are frightening once you're already inside them.
